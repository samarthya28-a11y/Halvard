## Not demonstrated (so not claimed)

- Dynamic detonation. Attachment verdicts are static analysis plus reputation; links are followed only in an offline model of the web. A document that is clean to static analysis passes until intelligence or the endpoint catches it (D1 depends on exactly that).
- A real Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace connector, its credentials and rate limits, and mail-flow rules in a real tenant. The reference platform defines the contract.
- Inline (pre-delivery) blocking. Courier acts after delivery; the measured gap in D1 is 6.5 minutes by construction of the scenario, not a measured production figure.
- Impersonation and phishing detection quality on real mail. Weights are hand-set; there is no measured detection or false-positive rate.
- F067 (file-sharing and third-party apps in collaboration suites), chat platforms, per-recipient behaviour baselines, a polling scheduler, and automatic expiry of pushed block-list hashes.
- Everything in the earlier phases' 'Not demonstrated' lists still applies (no native Windows/macOS sensor, no console, no asymmetric signing, no pen test).
