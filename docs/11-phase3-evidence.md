- Detection quality on real traffic. Thresholds are untuned; there are no measured detection or false-positive rates and no public test report.
- Continuous access evaluation, token lifetimes and token binding behave here as modelled; each real application and IdP must be checked for what it actually does after revocation.
- A polling scheduler: `process()` and `connector_health()` are called by tests. The 'within the promised interval' coverage latency for connectors is not demonstrated.
- Kerberos ticket lifetime and krbtgt rotation are described and refused-by-design, not exercised against a domain.
- Courier (mailed-attachment merge), Keeper approvers, console, installer, asymmetric signing and key management remain unbuilt, as do F057 decoys and F059 session recording.
- Case scoring is a documented heuristic; it has not been calibrated against real incidents.
