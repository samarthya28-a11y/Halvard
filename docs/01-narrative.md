# Halvard in one page

**For a founder who does not work in security.**

Your company runs on laptops, servers, email, cloud accounts and people's logins. Someone will eventually try to
get in, or someone inside will make a mistake. Three things then decide whether it is a bad afternoon or a bad year:
how fast harm is stopped, how small the damage stays, and how quickly work comes back. Afterwards, your board,
auditor, insurer and sometimes a regulator will ask you to prove it.

Halvard is one product that does those jobs together. It does not ask you to buy five tools and wire them up.

## What it does, in five outcomes

1. **Shrink what can be hit.** It shows what you have, what is exposed, and what to fix first.
2. **Stop active harm.** A small program on each computer (Ward) blocks bad behaviour on the device itself.
   It keeps working with no internet.
3. **Limit blast radius.** One action can isolate a laptop, lock the account, and pull the same mail from every inbox.
4. **Restore the work.** If files were scrambled, Halvard puts them back from its own record of what changed.
   It does not rely on Windows shadow copies, which attackers delete first.
5. **Prove it.** Every policy change, detection, approval and action goes into a tamper-evident record (Ledger).
   You hand an auditor evidence for one control without handing over your employees' documents.

## The board's four questions

*Are we covered?* *What is open?* *What did we stop?* *What evidence can we hand over?*
Halvard answers each in plain language. A computer that should be protected but is not counts as an open
problem, not a quiet gap in a chart.

## Who runs it

- A company with one IT person gets useful protection in one working day by following the in-product guide.
- A company with a security team gets hunting, custom detections and approved automation in the same console.
- A company with no one gets **Keeper**, a Halvard crew that works only inside a written list of what it may do.
- A group or service provider runs many companies from one place with hard walls between them.

## Promises we design to

- Prevention never waits for our servers.
- We collect what is needed to make security decisions. Not document contents, keystrokes, webcam or microphone.
- Every automatic action says who or what did it, why, how far it reached, who approved it, and how to undo it.
  When something cannot be undone, we say so before we do it.
- Your data is yours: documented export, and an uninstall that tells you what remains and for how long.
- We do not claim certifications we have not earned.

## What exists today (Phase 1)

A Windows-first guardian reference implementation with offline ransomware stop and file rollback, isolation,
quarantine, an approval-aware action layer, a tamper-evident audit record, tenant walls, a day-one setup
guide, and a performance ceiling that fails the build when exceeded. Everything beyond that is on the
roadmap and is not claimed. See `docs/07-phase1-evidence.md` for what was demonstrated and what was not.
